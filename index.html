The Purest Co.

DTC Supplements · Collagen Glow Berries · Product-level concepts

Two TOFU, problem-aware concepts for Collagen Glow Berries: postpartum breakouts and dull, tired-looking skin, with full hooks, scripts, and visual direction.

What I found

Avatars already tried topical-only fixes. The real tension is glow and firmness from within, plus feeling invisible or "tired" after kids and daily stress.

What I proposed

Mom-to-mom and "you look tired" opens, failed-path acknowledgment, product late, soft proof + 90-day risk reversal.

The insight it turned on Topical skincare fixed the surface. It didn't bring back the glow or firmness other people used to notice. So name that gap before the product shows up.

MarsMen

DTC Men's Health · Song ad concept · Problem-aware

Song ad for Ben, 35: office dad who lost his drive and keeps landing in the same place when he tries to change. Hooks, lyrics, month-by-month arc, CTA, and editor notes.

What I found

The tension is the loop, not the lab claim. Try to change, fail, feel emptier. Everyday recognition beats athlete aspiration for this avatar.

What I proposed

Dark-pop song ad. Product late. Day-1 doubt allowed. Gradual energy and presence with family over three months.

The insight it turned on "Where did that guy go?" lands harder than "boost your testosterone." Name the private loop first.

ON A LIVE ACCOUNT

First 30 days

What I would do if you put me on an account tomorrow.

01

Read the account

Winning ads, dead ads, comments, and the last 30-60 days of creative. Find what already works and what the customer is saying back.

02

Test the gap

Ship 3-5 concepts against the biggest open angle. Not more of the same winner, but the tension the current set is not naming.

03

Measure the right thing

Hook rate and thumb-stop first. Then CTR and CPA on the ones that hold attention. Kill fast, iterate on what shows signal.
