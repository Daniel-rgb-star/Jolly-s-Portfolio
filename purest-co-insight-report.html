Core Messaging System

Problem Agitation

  • Dull, dry, or sagging skin that shows age
  • Bloating and gut discomfort after meals
  • Stubborn weight / cravings
  • Complicated routines that don’t taste good

Emotional Payoff

  • Friends and family notice the glow
  • Feel like yourself again (postpartum / midlife)
  • Comfortable in your body
  • Actually look forward to taking the product

Winning Ad Patterns

1. “Friends / family noticed”

“People around us have noticed and asked what have we done.”

Why it wins: External validation beats self-reported results for beauty claims.

2. Taste + daily habit

“I actually look forward to having it”

Why it wins: Removes the biggest barrier to consistency in supplements.

3. Visible skin transformation

“Hydrated, glowy & plump”

Why it wins: Highest-volume customer outcome. Easy to desire and show.

4. 90-day guarantee + simple ritual

One sachet a day + risk-free trial

Why it wins: Lowers friction for higher-consideration beauty purchase.

Practical takeaway: Highest-performing Purest-style creatives lead with social proof (“people noticed”), pair it with a clear skin outcome, emphasise taste / easy habit, and close with dose transparency + 90-day guarantee.

Key Takeaways

Bottom line: The Purest Co wins by making clinically relevant actives (especially collagen) delicious and easy to take every day, then amplifying the social proof that “people noticed.” Sustained advantage depends on owning the “noticed by others + actually enjoyable” narrative.
