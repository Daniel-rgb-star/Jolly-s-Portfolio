PC

The Purest Co.

DTC Supplements · Collagen Glow Berries · Product-level concepts

Two TOFU, problem-aware concepts for Collagen Glow Berries: postpartum breakouts and dull, tired-looking skin, with full hooks, scripts, and visual direction.

What I found

Avatars already tried topical-only fixes. The real tension is glow and firmness from within, plus feeling invisible or "tired" after kids and daily stress.

What I proposed

Mom-to-mom and "you look tired" opens, failed-path acknowledgment, product late, soft proof + 90-day risk reversal.

The insight it turned on Topical skincare fixed the surface. It didn't bring back the glow or firmness other people used to notice. So name that gap before the product shows up.

MM

MarsMen

DTC Men's Health · Research, strategy, and creative concept

Two observations about their existing marketing, a reworked creative strategy, and a five scene concept built around the tension of lost drive for men in their thirties and forties.

What I found

Athlete and aspiration heavy creative sits on top of a quieter customer tension around energy and drive that is not always named out loud.

What I proposed

A problem first concept called "I Want My Drive Back" that lets the tension rise before the product shows up.

The insight it turned on Men in that age range often feel the problem before they have language for it. The ad should express the feeling first, then earn the product entrance.
