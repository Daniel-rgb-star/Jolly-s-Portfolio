CASE STUDY · DTC MEN'S HEALTH

MarsMen · Song Ad

Independent concept. Problem-aware song ad for men who feel their drive slipped and keep ending up in the same place when they try to change.

Setup

Avatar
Ben, 35. Father, office worker. Drinks, eats junk, lost his drive.
Awareness
Problem-aware · Market sophistication level 4
Format
Song ad · Pixar/Disney-style AI animation · 3–5s clips
Audio
AI song, dark pop (Billie Eilish-inspired), intimate vocal

Angle

After being down a long time, Ben decides to change. Every time he tries, he ends up in the same spot.

The tension is not "I need a testosterone supplement." It is the loop: try to change, fail, feel emptier. The product enters only after that loop is felt.

Hooks

  • Hook 1: Ben comes home from the office, disappointed, rethinking when he was 21 and still had energy for the sport he loved.
    Visual: Opens the door, stares at camera. Slow zoom. Cut to memory of younger Ben playing sport.
    "Where did that guy go? The one who never ran out of energy?"
  • Hook 2: Ben drinks a beer in front of the TV, looks at the can, and rethinks the person he used to be.
    Visual: Chair, beer, slow zoom into memory of past self, then back to present.
    "I keep saying tomorrow, but tomorrow looks the same..."

Editor notes

  • Style: Cinematic. Desaturated in Act 1, warming as Ben improves. Expressive face acting.
  • Character: Lock Ben's reference first (35, short dark hair, tired eyes that clear, office-casual). Same model every clip.
  • Pacing: 3–5 second clips on the beat. Faster in frustration. Slow in family and payoff.
  • Sound: Soft room tone, distant kids, keyboard, bottle, capsules. Vocals clear and intimate.
  • Claims: Energy, less afternoon crash, drive, presence with family. No extreme lab claims.
  • Tone: Honest and grounded. Emotion earned, not hyped.

Why this direction

Names the loop

Not "get your testosterone back." The private pattern: try to change, land in the same place.

Everyday Ben, not athlete

Office, beer, kids, afternoon crash. Recognition before aspiration.

Product late

MarsMen shows up after the loop is felt. Day-1 doubt is allowed. Progress is gradual.

Song format

Dark-pop intimacy + short animated cuts match scroll behavior and leave room for emotion before the CTA.
