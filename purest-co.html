CASE STUDY · DTC SUPPLEMENTS

The Purest Co.

Product-level creative for Collagen Glow Berries. Two TOFU, problem-aware concepts built from avatar research and competitor ad patterns — with full hooks, scripts, and visual direction.

Product & objective

Product: Collagen Glow Berries (The Purest Co.)

Build TOFU creative that speaks to women who feel their skin changed after kids or daily stress — dry, patchy, dull, or breakout-prone — and who have already tried topical skincare without getting glow or firmness from within.

Avatars

01

Postpartum mom (30–50)

After giving birth, dealing with breakouts. Wants the same youthful, glowy look she had before — and to feel more like herself again.

02

Fung, 35

Married, kids. Dry, patchy, dull skin that makes her look tired and flat by afternoon. Topicals helped the surface; not the glow or firmness others notice.

Core insight

Topical skincare fixed the surface. It didn't bring back the glow or firmness other people used to notice.

Both avatars already tried the obvious fix. The creative should respect that — name the failed path, then introduce support from within without sounding like another miracle claim.

Concept 1 · Postpartum breakouts

Avatar: Mom, 30–50, post-birth breakouts Angle: TOFU · Problem-aware · Relatable story

Hooks

  • Hook 1: "Mom-to-mom... what is going on with our skin lately?"
  • Hook 2: "After having a baby skin check"
  • Hook 3: "I survived labor, but my skin didn't"

Why these can work: specific call-out, pattern interrupt, and an open loop that invites the scroll-stop.

Body script

VO: I've been experiencing this lately…. I know.

Visual: after the line, show breakouts on her face.

VO: This started right after I had my son. Along the way, I just stopped prioritizing myself and put all my focus into motherhood and work.

VO: I'm so happy being a mom to my boy, but every time I look in the mirror, my breakouts make me feel so insecure. I just miss having clear, youthful-looking skin.

VO: It used to feel like everyone smiled at me when I walked around the mall, but lately, I feel practically invisible.

Visual: her not feeling the same — flat energy, avoiding the mirror.

VO: So I decided enough was enough and started taking care of myself again.

VO: Months of fixing my diet and routine, and I'm slowly getting my confidence back.

Visual: movement, hobbies, small routines that support how she wants to look.

VO: But I noticed breakouts still showing up. I realized relying on topical skincare wasn't enough.

VO: That's why I started taking Collagen Glow Berries from The Purest Co. and adding it to my routine.

Visual: clearer, glowy skin — less breakout, more like herself.

VO: It helped my skin look smooth and glowing again. I honestly feel like I'm back in my youth.

CTA: If you also want to start a daily routine that supports your skin — check out The Purest Co. Collagen Glow Berries.

Concept 2 · Dull, tired-looking skin

Avatar: Fung, 35 — dry, patchy, dull Angle: TOFU · Problem-aware · Brand-led proof

Hooks

  • Hook 1: "I was so sick of hearing ‘you look tired' when I was just sitting there with a bare face."
  • Hook 2: "Glowing in the morning, completely dull and flat by the afternoon"
  • Hook 3: "Before I figured this out, my skin constantly looked dry, patchy, and just... dull."

Angle idea: name the main problem without friction, then introduce the solution while leaning on brand credibility.

Scene 1 · Problem (10–15s)

VO: This is me throughout the day — I look tired and dull.

Visual: right after the hook — tired, dull face under real light.

VO: I tried to fix it with topical skincare, but it didn't help from within. Only on the surface.

Scene 2 · Solution (20–30s)

VO: Right when I was accepting this as my new look, my coworker recommended I start using Collagen Glow Berries from The Purest Co.

VO: Literally all it takes is three steps — rip, sip, and glow.

VO: This is me after just using it for four weeks. My face looks hydrated and radiant.

Visual: before/after of her face — clear, hydrated, more radiant.
Scene 3 · CTA

VO: I thought this was a hidden gem — but no. This is literally the #1 collagen serum in Singapore.

Visual: product + credibility cue (rank / social proof).

VO: Try Collagen Glow Berries risk-free. If you're still skeptical, they're running a 90-day money-back guarantee.

Why this direction

Specific over generic

Mom-to-mom and "you look tired" beat category-wide claims. The viewer can point at themselves in the first three seconds.

Failed path acknowledged

Both scripts admit topical-only didn't deliver glow or firmness. That matches how this audience already thinks.

Product enters late

Problem and story first. Collagen Glow Berries shows up after the tension is real — not as the opening claim.

Soft proof + risk reversal

Brand rank + 90-day guarantee handle residual skepticism without hard-sell language.
