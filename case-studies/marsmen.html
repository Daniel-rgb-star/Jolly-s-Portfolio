CASE STUDY

MarsMen

DTC Men's Health & Wellness — Research, Strategy & Creative Concept

Full independent research into dosing friction and creator relationships, a reworked creative strategy, and a complete 5-scene concept built to test.

Customer research

I looked at the target audience's emotional state, the two biggest purchase barriers (dosing complexity and trust in creators), the tension between aspiration and relatability, and the real insight behind why people actually buy.

Creative concept: "I Want My Drive Back"

A 5-scene concept designed to be tested directly against their current creative.

Scene structure

1. Hook — Emotional recognition of lost energy/drive.

2. The friction — Why most solutions feel complicated or untrustworthy.

3. The simple path — Clear, low-friction offer.

4. Proof — Relatable results.

5. Close — Direct CTA focused on getting drive back.

This concept prioritizes emotional clarity and reduced friction over feature lists.

What this shows

I can research a real brand, identify the real customer barriers, and turn that into a testable creative concept with a clear hypothesis.
